Refund policy

Purchases and returns.

Physical Books

Physical books may be returned within 14 days of delivery if in original, undamaged condition. Contact Publisher@JackrabbitPunkinPublishing.com with order information. Customer is responsible for return shipping unless the item was received damaged or incorrect.

Signed Books

Signed and personalized books are final sale. Due to their personalized nature, signed books cannot be returned or exchanged.

Digital Products

All digital product sales are final. Refunds are not provided once a digital product has been accessed or downloaded.

Shipping policy

Processing and delivery.

Processing Times

Orders are processed within 3–5 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing the next business day. Signed and personalized books may require additional processing time.

Shipping Methods

  • Standard Shipping: 5–7 business days (domestic)
  • Expedited Shipping: 2–3 business days (domestic, where available)
  • International Shipping: available on select orders—contact us for rates

Lost Package Procedure

If your package does not arrive within the expected timeframe, check your tracking information first. If marked delivered but not received, or if tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days, contact us immediately. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expected delivery date.

Accessibility statement

Access for all visitors.

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to all visitors, including those with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Our efforts include clear page structure and headings, sufficient color contrast, alt text for all images, keyboard navigation support, and mobile-optimized design.

If you experience difficulty accessing content, contact Publisher@JackrabbitPunkinPublishing.com. We are committed to responding promptly.
