Refund Policy

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC values every customer and strives to provide quality books and products. Please review this policy carefully before placing an order.

Physical Books

All physical book sales are final.

Due to the nature of independent publishing, small-batch inventory management, print-on-demand fulfillment, and personalized author services, Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC does not accept returns or provide refunds for physical books once an order has been processed and shipped.

Refunds or exchanges will not be issued for:

  • Buyer’s remorse
  • Accidental purchases
  • Duplicate orders
  • Incorrect title selections made by the customer
  • Shipping delays outside our control
  • Books delivered as ordered

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you receive an incorrect title, please contact us within seven (7) days of delivery.

Please include:

  • Full name
  • Order number
  • Description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the damaged, defective, or incorrect item
  • Photograph of the shipping package if available

Upon verification, Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC may provide a replacement copy at no additional cost. If a replacement cannot reasonably be provided, a refund may be issued at the sole discretion of Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC.

Signed & Personalized Books

All sales of signed, personalized, inscribed, or special-order books are final.

These items cannot be returned, exchanged, or refunded under any circumstances. Please verify all personalization information carefully before placing your order.

Digital Products

All digital product sales are final.

Due to the nature of digital content, refunds are not available once a product has been downloaded, accessed, delivered, or transmitted electronically.

Third-Party Retailers

Books purchased through Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Ingram distribution partners, bookstores, online retailers, or other third-party sellers are subject to the return and refund policies of the retailer from which the purchase was made.

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC cannot process refunds, returns, exchanges, or credits for purchases made through third-party retailers.

Print-on-Demand Fulfillment

Some orders may be fulfilled through third-party printing and distribution partners, including but not limited to Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, or other fulfillment providers.

When an order is fulfilled through a third-party provider, Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC will assist customers with verified claims involving damaged, defective, or incorrect items when possible. Any replacement or refund remains subject to verification and provider policies.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information at checkout. Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC is not responsible for delays, losses, or returned packages resulting from incorrect or incomplete shipping information provided by the customer.

Accessibility Statement

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We believe that everyone deserves equal access to information, and we continually work to improve the accessibility and usability of our site.

Our Commitment

We strive to meet or exceed the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

Accessibility Features

Our ongoing accessibility efforts include:

  • Clear, consistent page structure and logical heading hierarchy
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background elements
  • Descriptive alternative text for all meaningful images
  • Keyboard navigation support throughout the website
  • Mobile-optimized, responsive design for all screen sizes
  • No auto-playing audio or video content
  • Clear, readable font sizes throughout
  • Descriptive link text that makes sense out of context

Known Limitations

While we work diligently to ensure accessibility, some content or features may not yet fully meet all WCAG 2.1 Level AA success criteria. We are actively working to identify and address these limitations. If you encounter a specific barrier, please contact us and we will do our best to provide the information in an accessible format.
