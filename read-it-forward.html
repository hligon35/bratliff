The Read It Forward Program

Books are tools for transformation. Help put meaningful stories into the hands that need them most.

Our mission

A book can open a door.

The Read It Forward Program is the heart of Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC.

We serve veterans, schools, youth organizations, community centers, and underserved neighborhoods—anywhere a book can open a door that might otherwise remain closed. Through sponsorships, partnerships, and community support, we place meaningful books into the hands of veterans, service members, schools, and organizations that can benefit from their message.

Who we serve

Reading reaches every community.

  • Veterans and active-duty military personnel
  • Public schools and school libraries
  • Community centers and neighborhood organizations
  • Youth and after-school programs
  • Faith communities and houses of worship
  • Correctional and rehabilitation facilities
  • Public libraries in underserved areas

How it works

From sponsor to community.

  1. Sponsor books through Read It Forward.
  2. We prepare and distribute the books.
  3. Partner organizations identify recipients.
  4. Books are placed directly into communities.
  5. Sponsors receive impact updates.

Partnership opportunities
