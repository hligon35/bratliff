Introduction

A compelling voice shaped by service.

Barbara J. Ratliff draws from more than two decades of military service, her work with veterans, her journey as an author and publisher, and a lifetime of lessons shaped by faith, family, resilience, and service.

Whether addressing a keynote audience, joining a panel discussion, leading a workshop, visiting a book club, or sitting down for an interview, Barbara brings lived experience, thoughtful conversation, and a genuine desire to leave audiences with something meaningful to carry forward.
