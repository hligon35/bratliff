RESOURCE CENTER

At Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing, we believe a meaningful story should do more than sit on a shelf. It should encourage conversation, inspire learning, strengthen communities, and create opportunities for others.

The JPP Resource Center extends the impact of our books through thoughtfully developed materials for readers, book clubs, educators, families, literacy partners, community organizations, authors, and aspiring independent publishers.

Whether you're continuing a conversation sparked by one of our books, helping a young reader explore a story more deeply, supporting literacy in your community, or learning more about the publishing journey, there is a place for you here.

Explore the resources. Continue the conversation. Carry the story forward.
