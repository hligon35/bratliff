Battles Beyond the Waves

Barbara J. RatliffMilitary Memoir / Nonfiction

The story of a soldier, a survivor, and a woman of extraordinary faith and resilience.

Barbara J. Ratliff takes readers inside the experience of military service—the camaraderie, the sacrifice, the pride, and the pain—and follows that journey through the battles that come after: the invisible wounds, the difficult transitions, and the ongoing fight for healing and recognition.

Written with honesty and grace, this book is essential reading for veterans, families of service members, and anyone who wants to understand the full cost and meaning of service to country.
