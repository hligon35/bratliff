Short bio

Barbara J. Ratliff

Barbara J. Ratliff is a veteran, author, and publisher. She is a co-founder of Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC and the author of Battles Beyond the Waves.

Through her work and Read It Forward, a JPP initiative developed by Barbara and Charles Ratliff, she champions literacy, community, and the enduring power of storytelling.

Long bio

Barbara J. Ratliff is a United States military veteran, author, publisher, and community advocate. As a co-founder and publisher of Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC, she has dedicated her post-service career to bringing meaningful, authentic stories to readers who need them. Her debut memoir, Battles Beyond the Waves, draws on her military experience to illuminate the realities of service and the challenges of life after the military.

Read It Forward is a JPP initiative developed by Barbara and Charles Ratliff, with Charles's volunteer work supporting reading at his daughter's elementary school connected to its origin. Her speaking topics include resilience, veterans' stories, women in the military, storytelling, faith, purpose, literacy, and publishing.
