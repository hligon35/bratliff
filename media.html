Short bio

Barbara J. Ratliff

Barbara J. Ratliff is a veteran, author, and publisher. She is the founder of Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC and the author of Battles Beyond the Waves.

Through her work and the Read It Forward Program, Barbara champions literacy, community, and the enduring power of storytelling.

Long bio

Barbara J. Ratliff is a United States military veteran, author, publisher, and community advocate. As the founder and publisher of Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC, she has dedicated her post-service career to bringing meaningful, authentic stories to readers who need them. Her debut memoir, Battles Beyond the Waves, draws on her military experience to illuminate the realities of service and the challenges of life after the military.

Barbara founded the Read It Forward Program to place books in underserved communities, veteran organizations, and schools across the country. She is a sought-after speaker on resilience, veterans' stories, women in the military, and the power of publishing.
