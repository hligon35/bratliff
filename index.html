At Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing, every book begins with a purpose. Whether preserving history, sharing stories of resilience, inspiring young readers, or supporting veterans, our mission is to create books that strengthen communities and leave a lasting impact.

Welcome

Books can be promises.

Jackrabbit Punkin Publishing LLC was founded with a singular purpose: to bring meaningful, authentic stories to readers who need them. We publish books that speak to the human experience—stories of resilience, faith, community, and legacy.

Every title in our catalog is chosen not just for its literary merit, but for the impact it can have on the reader. Whether you are a veteran navigating life after service, a student discovering the power of words, or a community leader looking for tools to inspire—we publish for you.

Our books are more than products. They are promises to preserve truth, honor sacrifice, and keep the stories of extraordinary people alive for generations to come.

Barbara J. RatliffMilitary Memoir

Barbara J. Ratliff's powerful military memoir—an honest, unflinching account of service, survival, and the battles that continue long after homecoming.

With compassion and courage, Barbara invites readers into a world most will never see, honoring the experiences of veterans and shedding light on the hidden costs of service. This is a book for every American who has ever wondered what it truly means to serve.

The Read It Forward Program
